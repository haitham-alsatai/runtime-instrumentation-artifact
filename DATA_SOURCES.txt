Public benchmark sources used by this artifact
================================================

RCAEval
https://github.com/phamquiluan/RCAEval

The Stage A experiments use the RE2-OB (Online Boutique) and RE2-TT
(Train Ticket) portions. Place the extracted RE2 directory at:

data/RE2/

GAIA-DataSet
https://github.com/CloudWise-OpenSource/GAIA-DataSet

The Stage B integrated experiment uses the July 2021 GAIA MicroSS traces,
metrics, and run table. See data/GAIA/README.md for the expected layout.

Raw datasets are not redistributed in this repository. Derived outputs needed
to inspect and verify the reported results are included under results/.
